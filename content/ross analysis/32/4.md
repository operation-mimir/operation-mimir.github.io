## Supply the induction argument needed in the proof of [[ross analysis/32/lem 32.2|lem 32.2]].

Continuing the proof of [[ross analysis/32/lem 32.2|lem 32.2]], we have shown that the relation $L(f,P) \leq L(f,Q)$ when $Q$ has one point more than $P$.

Assume this result is true when $Q$ has $k$ points more than $P$. We shall denote this $Q$ by $Q_k$ to emphasize the fact that it has $k$ more points and write

$$
L(f,P) \leq L(f,Q_k) \tag{1}
$$

Now let $Q_{k+1}$ be a partition with 1 point more than $Q$ (and $k+1$ point more than $P$). Using the result for 1 more point

$$
L(f,Q_k) \leq L(f,Q_{k+1}) \tag{2}
$$

From $(1)$ and $(2)$ we get the result for $k+1$ points

$$
L(f,P) \leq L(f,Q_{k+1})
$$

And thus induction is complete.

$$
\ \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
$$
