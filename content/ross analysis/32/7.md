## Let $f$ be integrable on $[a,b]$, and suppose $g$ is a function on $[a,b]$ such that $g(x)=f(x)$ except for finitely many $x$ in $[a,b]$. Show $g$ is integrable and $\int^b_af = \int^b_ag$. Hint: First reduce to the case where $f$ is the function identically equal to 0.
