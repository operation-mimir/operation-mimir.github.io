## Repeat [[ross analysis/32/2|Exercise 32.2]] for $g$ where $g(x)=x^2$ for rational $x$ and $g(x)=0$ for irrational $x$.

Let $P=\{0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=b\}$ be a partition of $[0,b]$.

In any interval $[t_{k-1},t_k]$ for $k\in\{1,2,\ldots n\}$, $M(f,[t_{k-1},t_k])=t_k^2$ and $m(f,[t_{k-1},t_k])=0$.

Thus by [[ross analysis/32/def 32.1|def 32.1]]

$$
U(f,P)=\sum_{k=1}^n t_k^2(t_k-t_{k-1})
$$

and

$$
L(f,P)=0
$$

If we choose the partition $P$ such that $t_k=\frac{kb}{n}$, then

$$
U(f,P)=\sum_{k=1}^n \left(\frac{kb}{n}\right)^2\left(\frac{b}{n}\right)=\left(\frac{b}{n}\right)^3\sum_{k=1}^n k^2=\frac{b^3}{n^3}\cdot\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}
$$

Now $U(f,P)>\frac{b^3}{3}$ so $U(f) \geq \frac{b^3}{3}$.

Also

$$
U(f,P)\geq\left(\frac{b}{n}\right)^3\sum_{k=1}^n (k-1)^2 = \frac{b^3}{n^3}\cdot\frac{n(n-1)(2n-1)}{6}=\frac{b^3}{3}\left(1-\frac{1}{n}\right)\left(1-\frac{1}{2n}\right) = \frac{b^3}{3}\left(1 - \frac{3}{2n} + \frac{1}{2n^2}\right)
$$

Now $U(f,P) \geq \frac{b^3}{3}\left(1- \frac{3}{2n} + \frac{1}{2n^2}\right)$ so $U(f) \geq \frac{b^2}{2}\left(1- \frac{3}{2n} + \frac{1}{2n^2}\right)$.

Rearranging the terms

$$
\frac{b^3}{3} - U(f) \leq \frac{b^3}{3}\left(\frac{3}{2n} - \frac{1}{2n^2}\right) = \frac{b^3}{3}\frac{1}{2n}\left(3-\frac{1}{n}\right) \leq \frac{b^3}{2n}
$$

But $U(f) - \frac{b^3}{3} \geq 0$ since $U(f) \geq \frac{b^3}{3}$.

Thus $U(f) = \frac{b^3}{3}$.

So $U(f)\neq L(f)$ and $f$ is not integrable.
